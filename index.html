A FEW IDEAS FOR YOUR NEXT BITE

Local food to try

Explore Filipino favorites, from savory chicken adobo to tangy sinigang and crispy lumpia.

SAVORY FAVORITE

CHICKEN · FILIPINO CLASSIC

Chicken adobo

Chicken braised with soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and seasonings until tender and full of savory flavor. Often served with rice.
TANGY COMFORT

PORK · SOUR SOUP

Pork sinigang

A comforting Filipino soup with tender pork, vegetables, and a pleasantly sour broth, commonly flavored with tamarind.
CRISPY FAVORITE

CRISPY · SHAREABLE SNACK

Filipino lumpia

Thin wrappers fried until crisp around a savory filling, often served with a sweet-and-sour dipping sauce.

CHEF SPOTLIGHT · PROFILE

Meet Chef Aniela Aguilar

Cook, market regular, and believer in sharing a good meal.

Aniela builds her menus around what local growers bring in each week. Her cooking pairs familiar family recipes with fresh, seasonal ingredients—simple food made with care.

“The best dish starts with good ingredients and gets better when you share it.”
